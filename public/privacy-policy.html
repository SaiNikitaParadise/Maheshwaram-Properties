    Privacy Policy

    How Maheshwaram Properties respects and safeguards your personal data and privacy.

    Home› Privacy Policy
    Last Updated: October 2026

    1. Introduction

    Welcome to Maheshwaram Properties ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of our clients, website visitors, and prospective buyers across South Hyderabad and beyond.

    This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website (maheshwaramproperties.in), inquire about our plotted ventures, or interact with our sales and advisory team.

    2. Information We Collect

    We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

    • Submit an inquiry form for any of our DTCP / HMDA approved ventures.
    • Schedule or request a site visit.
    • Contact us via WhatsApp, phone calls, or email.
    • Subscribe to real estate updates, market newsletters, or brochures.

    The types of information collected may include:

    • Contact Information: Full name, phone number, mobile/WhatsApp number, email address.
    • Property Requirements: Preferred venture locations (Maheshwaram, Mansanpally, Kandukur, Kadthal, etc.), budget range, and timeline for investment.
    • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected through standard web server logs and analytics cookies.

    3. How We Use Your Information

    Your information is used strictly to provide you with the best possible real estate advisory service, including:

    • Responding promptly to your inquiries regarding plot pricing, availability, and layout approvals.
    • Arranging and coordinating personalized site visits with our local property experts.
    • Sharing official layout copies (HMDA/DTCP L.P. numbers), brochures, and location maps.
    • Providing customer support throughout your property selection, documentation, and registration process.
    • Improving website performance, usability, and visitor experience.

    4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

    We value your trust. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing agencies.

    We may only share your information in the following limited situations:

    • Authorized Property Advisors: Direct team members and authorized representatives dedicated to addressing your venture request.
    • Legal Compliance: When required by applicable Indian laws, judicial proceedings, or statutory regulatory authorities.

    5. Data Security

    We employ administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. While no internet transmission is 100% secure, we continuously maintain industry-standard security protocols to protect your data.

    6. Cookies & Analytics

    Our website utilizes cookies and similar technologies (such as Google Tag Manager / Google Analytics) to analyze web traffic, track user engagement, and enhance user experience. You can choose to accept or decline cookies through your browser settings at any time.

    7. Third-Party Links & Services

    Our website may include links to external services, such as WhatsApp for instant chat communication, Google Maps for venture navigation, or YouTube for video walkthroughs. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external third-party platforms.

    8. Your Rights & Preferences

    You have the right to:

    • Request details on the personal data we hold about you.
    • Request correction or updating of any inaccurate contact details.
    • Opt out of future property notifications or promotional communications at any time.

    9. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

    If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, please contact our team directly:
