About Cyber Space

Cyber Space is a DTCP-approved residential land development located in the Nandupally Grampanchayat area of Nagaram, Maheshwaram, Ranga Reddy District, Hyderabad. Thoughtfully developed under the residential zone classification, this project offers clear-title plots that give buyers the confidence of legally sound, hassle-free ownership.

The project takes its name from its enviable location at the nexus of Hyderabad's technology-driven growth — positioned in Nagaram, Maheshwaram, where the convergence of the ORR, Shamshabad Airport, and Hyderabad's expanding IT and Pharma corridors has created one of the most dynamic real estate micro-markets in the region.

Cyber Space is designed for discerning buyers who seek legally clear, residentially zoned land in a rapidly appreciating location. With clear title deeds, full DTCP compliance, and a prime location in the airport–ORR corridor, Cyber Space offers investors a rare combination of legal security, strategic positioning, and strong appreciation potential in Hyderabad's fast-growing South zone.
