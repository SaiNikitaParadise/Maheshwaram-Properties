About Sai Kshethra

Sai Kshethra is a distinguished residential plot development in Maheshwaram, Hyderabad, developed by ESR Projects (also known as Amaze Infra). Spanning approximately 10–15 acres and comprising 57 thoughtfully demarcated plots, it is an exclusive community that prioritizes premium living in a secure and well-connected environment.

Plot dimensions range from 173 to 762 sq. yards, catering to diverse family requirements — from compact, efficient homes to grand, spacious villas. Every plot benefits from meticulously planned infrastructure: a distinctive entrance arch, blacktop roads, underground electrical cabling for a clutter-free streetscape, and a reliable piped water supply system.

Sai Kshethra's strategic location provides exceptional access to Hyderabad's major employment and lifestyle hubs — quick connectivity to the Outer Ring Road (ORR), NH 765, HITEC City, and the Financial District positions it as both a sound investment and a premium residential destination.
