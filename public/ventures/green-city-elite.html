About Green City Elite

Green City Elite is a DTCP-approved residential land development located in Nagaram, Maheshwaram, developed during 2006–2008. Classified under the R1 Residential Zone, this well-established layout has been a sought-after address for families and investors who value legal clarity, prime location, and long-term appreciation potential.

The project's proximity to ORR Exit No. 15 and Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, Shamshabad, makes it one of the most strategically positioned residential layouts in the Maheshwaram micro-market. Plots of approximately 200 sq. yds. offer an ideal footprint for spacious independent home construction.

With the layout now mature and fully developed, a healthy resale market has emerged — making Green City Elite an excellent opportunity for both end-users seeking a ready-infrastructure address and investors looking for premium, fully-appreciated land in the airport corridor zone.
