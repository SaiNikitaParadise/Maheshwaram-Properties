About Song of the South

Song of the South is a spacious residential plotted development spread across Dubbacherla village in Maheshwaram Mandal, Ranga Reddy District. Laid out over roughly 30 acres, the venture offers around 322 plots ranging from 240 to 800 square yards, giving buyers flexibility to choose a plot size that suits their needs — whether a compact residential plot or a larger one for a custom-built villa.

The layout is planned with wide internal roads of 100 feet, 60 feet, and 40 feet, giving the community a spacious, well-organized feel with room for future landscaping and utilities. The venture carries HMDA approval, giving buyers the regulatory clarity that's essential for long-term investment and home construction.

Dubbacherla sits along the South Zone growth corridor of Hyderabad, close to Mansanpally and within easy reach of the Outer Ring Road (ORR) and the upcoming Regional Ring Road (RRR). This positions Song of the South well for buyers looking at both end-use and long-term appreciation as South Hyderabad's residential and industrial corridors continue to expand.
