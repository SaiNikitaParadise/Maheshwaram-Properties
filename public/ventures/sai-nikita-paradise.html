About Sai Nikita Paradise

Sai Nikita Paradise, developed by Sai Nikita Estates Pvt. Ltd., is an exclusive 13.65-acre residential gated community located in Thummloor, Maheshwaram Mandal, Ranga Reddy District. The project offers 149 premium residential plots ranging from 1800 to 5400 sq. ft., giving buyers flexibility to choose the perfect canvas for their dream home.

Designed with a resort-lifestyle philosophy, Sai Nikita Paradise integrates lush landscaped gardens, a fully equipped gymnasium, clubhouse, swimming pool, and jogging track — all within a secured gated perimeter. Rainwater harvesting and a sewage treatment plant ensure ecological responsibility while power backup guarantees uninterrupted living.

The project enjoys financing support from leading banks including HDFC, AXIS, ICICI, Kotak, Yes Bank, and SBI — making home ownership more accessible than ever. Its prime location in Thummloor, Maheshwaram provides seamless access to the Outer Ring Road, Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, and the growing IT and Pharma corridors of South Hyderabad.
