About Shreya Green Meadows

Shreya Green Meadows is an HMDA and TG-RERA approved premium residential plotted development by Shreya Infra Group, spread across roughly 12 acres at Mansanpally X Road, Maheshwaram Mandal, Ranga Reddy District. The layout is officially sanctioned under HMDA permit LP No. 11/LO/Plg/HMDA/2019 and Telangana RERA registration P02400000550, offering complete legal peace-of-mind to buyers and investors.

The project comprises 146 thoughtfully planned residential plots ranging from 141 to 230 sq. yds, laid out around wide black-topped avenues, underground drainage, and generous open green spaces. Designed as a nature-inspired gated community, Shreya Green Meadows blends premium infrastructure with a peaceful, low-density living experience — ideal for building a dream home or holding as a long-term investment.

Mansanpally is one of the fastest-growing pockets of South Hyderabad, benefiting from proximity to Maheshwaram, Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, the Outer Ring Road (ORR), Srisailam Highway and upcoming employment hubs including Pharma City and Fab City. The location's rapid infrastructure upgrades continue to drive strong capital appreciation year after year.
