About Chalapathi Garden

Chalapathi Garden is one of Maheshwaram's most historically significant and trusted residential plot ventures. Developed and approved under LP Number 34/2002/H, this community has been serving homebuyers and investors since 2002 — making it a pioneer in the Mansanpally real estate landscape.

Located in the Mansanpally Revenue Village of Maheshwaram Mandal, Ranga Reddy District, Chalapathi Garden has stood the test of time as a reliable, legally sound, and infrastructure-ready gated community. The venture offers plots in various sizes typically ranging from 200 to 400 sq. yds, catering to a broad spectrum of buyers.

With over two decades of proven track record, Chalapathi Garden has become a benchmark for quality plotted development in South Hyderabad. The community's proximity to the International Airport (Shamshabad), the ORR, and the upcoming Hyderabad Pharma City has resulted in consistent and strong appreciation for plot owners over the years.
