About Chalapathi Melbourne Kadthal

Chalapathi Melbourne Kadthal is a flagship 82-acre plotted township by Chalapathi Estates Pvt. Ltd., located on the fast-developing Srisailam Highway at Kadthal, Ranga Reddy District. Positioned as a ready-to-move community with approximately 2,000 planned residential units, the venture stands out for its scale, master-planning and full DTCP & RERA compliance — the highest degree of legal assurance for investors and end-users.

Every plot within the township is served by black-top (BT) internal roads, underground drainage (UGD), underground electrical cabling, overhead water storage and lush avenue plantation that creates beautiful tree-lined streetscapes. The layout is complemented by a compound wall, entrance arch, landscaped parks and dedicated open spaces — reflecting the "world-class residential project" standard Chalapathi Estates is known for.

Kadthal's rapid growth is powered by its position on the Srisailam Highway, direct access to Hyderabad Pharma City (Asia's largest pharma cluster), and quick reach to the Bangalore Highway and Outer Ring Road. This connectivity, combined with the scale and approvals of Chalapathi Melbourne, makes it one of the most compelling long-term investment destinations in South Hyderabad.
