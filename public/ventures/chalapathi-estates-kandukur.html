    About Chalapathi Estates Kandukur

    Chalapathi Estates Kandukur, developed under the flagship "Unique City Annexe" project, is a DTCP-approved residential layout by Chalapathi Estates Pvt. Ltd. — a company with over two decades of excellence in South Hyderabad real estate, founded in 2001 by Y.V. Anjaneyulu. The project is located in Meerkhanpet Village, Kandukur Mandal, Ranga Reddy District, Hyderabad.

    Plots start from 2304 sq. ft and are laid out in a thoughtful gated community style, giving residents the exclusivity and security of a gated neighbourhood with the spaciousness of independent plot ownership. The community features beautifully laid black-top (BT) roads, lush avenue plantation for a green canopy effect, and a modern underground drainage (UGD) system.

    All plots in Unique City Annexe carry clear, encumbrance-free titles — a hallmark of Chalapathi Estates' commitment to legal transparency. The location on the Srisailam Highway corridor ensures excellent long-term appreciation potential, with easy access to schools, hospitals, commercial establishments, and transportation networks.
