    About Meher Crown City

    Meher Crown City (also known as Meher's Sreecons Crown) is a prestigious DTCP-approved residential layout strategically positioned in Maheshwaram Town, Ranga Reddy District, Hyderabad. The project stands out for its exceptionally wide internal road network — master roads of 60 feet, secondary roads of 40 feet, and residential roads of 33 feet — ensuring a spacious, free-flowing layout that values every resident's convenience and mobility.

    Located adjacent to Maheshwaram Town and within direct reach of ORR Exit No. 14 and the Srisailam Highway, Meher Crown City occupies one of Maheshwaram's most premium addresses. Residents enjoy the dual benefit of a tranquil, green residential setting while being minutes away from the airport, major IT parks, and high-growth commercial zones.

    The layout's proximity to Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, Fab City, TCS Adibatla Campus, and Ramky Discovery City positions it at the intersection of Hyderabad's three biggest growth corridors — the airport corridor, the IT-SEZ belt, and the pharma-life sciences cluster — making it an outstanding long-term investment opportunity.
