About Rich Green Bagh Mankhal

Rich Green Bagh Mankhal is an HMDA approved premium residential plotted venture situated in Baghmankhal (Mankhal), on the fast-appreciating southeast corridor of Hyderabad. Designed as a gated plotted community, the layout offers clear-title HMDA plots with well-defined boundaries, wide black-topped internal roads, underground drainage, water and electrical utilities, and lush avenue plantation lining every street.

The venture is planned for buyers who want the assurance of full HMDA approval combined with proximity to Hyderabad's premier IT and lifestyle destinations. With plot options suitable for a range of budgets and family sizes, Rich Green Bagh Mankhal is equally attractive to end-users building their dream home and to investors targeting a high-growth corridor.

Mankhal / Baghmankhal enjoys quick connectivity to the Outer Ring Road (ORR), Adibatla IT hub (TCS, TATA), Hardware Park, Fab City, Wonderla, and the upcoming Regional Ring Road (RRR). This convergence of employment, infrastructure and entertainment nodes has established Mankhal as one of the strongest growth micro-markets in South-East Hyderabad.
