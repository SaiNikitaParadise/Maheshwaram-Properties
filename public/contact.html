Frequently Asked Questions

Are your plots DTCP or HMDA approved? +

Yes, all our ventures including Chalapathi Paradise, Chalapathi Garden, Karvy Nova, Vision Meadows Seasons, and Palm Meadows carry full DTCP (Directorate of Town and Country Planning) or HMDA approvals. We share all approval documents transparently before any purchase.

Can I take a home loan for purchasing a plot? +

Yes! We have tie-ups with leading banks and NBFCs that offer plot loans and composite loans (plot + construction). Our finance desk will help you identify the best option and expedite the approval process.

How far is Maheshwaram from Hyderabad city? +

Maheshwaram is approximately 30–35 km from Hyderabad city centre. With access to the Outer Ring Road (ORR), it is only about 30–40 minutes from most major areas of the city. The Rajiv Gandhi International Airport is just 15 minutes away.

Can NRIs purchase plots through Maheshwaram Properties? +

Absolutely! We have a dedicated NRI desk that handles virtual site tours, power of attorney arrangements, FEMA-compliant documentation and remote registration support. Many of our satisfied clients are NRIs from the US, UK, Gulf countries and Australia.

What is the minimum plot size available? +

Plot sizes vary across our ventures, typically starting from 100 sq. yds going up to 500+ sq. yds. Chalapathi Garden, for example, offers plots from 200–400 sq. yds. Contact us to know the current availability across all ventures.
